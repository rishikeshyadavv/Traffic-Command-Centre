Route optimizer

Cost formula

cost = length/speed × (1 + 0.6 × (V/C)³ + 0.5 × cellDensity). V = vehicles on road + predicted load; V/C capped at 3. Closed roads are excluded, not penalised.

Dispatch

How it works

Units use their own cost model (1.4x speed, weak congestion penalty) with the selected algorithm (A* or Dijkstra) and are re-planned every 3 s, so they are redirected around closures and jams. Cars ahead on the same road pull over (yellow ring); cars about to enter the unit's path are diverted by the load-balanced assignment with a x6 penalty on those roads.
