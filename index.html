Route optimizer

Cost formula

cost = length/speed x (1 + 0.6 x (V/C)^3 + 0.5 x cellDensity). V = vehicles on road + predicted load; V/C capped at 3. Closed roads are excluded, not penalised.

Dispatch

How it works

Units are routed with their own cost model (1.4x speed, weak congestion penalty) using the selected algorithm (A* or Dijkstra) and re-planned every 3 s, so they are redirected around closures and jams. Cars ahead on the same road pull over (yellow ring); cars about to enter the unit's path are diverted by the load-balanced assignment with a x6 penalty on those roads.
